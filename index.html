📊 Portfolio Dashboard

Portfolio management with project health tracking, financial reporting, operational analytics, and a Smart Assistant.

This is a portfolio demo. All data (projects, alerts, financials, tasks) is fictional sample data, seeded on first load. Everything is editable — add, edit and delete projects, alerts, revenue months, budget allocations and tasks. Edits save to your browser only (localStorage); nothing is ever sent to a server. Use "Reset demo data" to restore the original demo seed.
